    Changes are sent to the keyboard as you make them.

    To change the lighting, connect the USB cable. Your K980 is connected through the 2.4G dongle, which only carries key presses — lighting can only be set over the cable (Xinmeng's own driver works the same way).
    1. Plug the USB cable into the keyboard and this PC.
    2. Slide the keyboard's mode switch to wired / USB.
    3. The controls below unlock by themselves. What you choose is saved in the keyboard and stays in 2.4G and Bluetooth mode.
