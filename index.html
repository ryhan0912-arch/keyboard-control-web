    Music mode

    The keys light up with the sound while this page is open (Chrome may keep it running in the background). Stop it to get your saved lighting back.

    “PC sound (automatic)” hears whatever this computer plays on the device you're using right now — speakers, headphones or monitor — and follows you when you switch, with no permission pop-ups. It needs the small music helper running on this PC: download K980-Music-Helper.cmd, double-click it and leave its window open (minimized is fine). The helper drives the lights itself, so music mode keeps running after you close this page; open the page again to change or stop it. It only listens to the sound on this PC and sends nothing anywhere else. Music mode needs the keyboard on the USB cable (wired mode).

    Connect the keyboard with the USB cable in wired mode to use music mode.
